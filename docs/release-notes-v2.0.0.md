## v2.0.0 — redesigned interface

The whole app has a new layout and look. Searches, prices and everything else work the
same way; your saved builds, basket, history and settings carry over.

**New layout**
- A **sidebar** holds your build (gear set, save, switch build, compare with character)
  and the pages: Gear, Jewels & clusters, Flasks, Gems, Upgrade planner, Timeless jewels,
  Jewel finder, Market.
- Gear, jewels and flasks show a **slot list** beside one item at a time, with each slot's
  price or status (Bought, None listed, Equipped, Missing). No more scrolling through every
  card. Arrow keys move through the list.
- The **top bar** shows the search settings in use; change them under **Search settings ▾**.
- A new **start screen**: paste box, saved builds as one-click cards, and how it works.

**Item cards**
- **Check price**, **Go live** and **Search on trade** sit next to the item name; Copy URL,
  Craft vs buy, Find seeds and Mark bought are under **⋯**.
- The less-used filters (Search by, Match, ilvl, sockets, links, min roll, max price) fold
  into **More filters**, which shows how many you've changed.
- Listings show the price bigger and one main button: **Go to hideout** for instant
  buyout, **Whisper** for in-person. Pin, Copy whisper and /hideout are under **⋯**.

**Tool pages**
- **Upgrade planner**: build cost and the basket as sortable tables (click an item to jump
  to its slot), plus the budget solver.
- **Timeless jewels** and **Jewel finder** read as numbered steps. The jewel finder has one
  **Off / Want / Must** switch per mod instead of two checkboxes.
- **Market**: a large 7-day Divine Orb chart above the currency table.
- **Settings** opens as a panel on the right, grouped into Appearance, Accessibility,
  Alerts, In game, Account (your POESESSID now lives here) and Data.

**Look and accessibility**
- **Dark** and **Light** themes (or match Windows) with five accent colours, replacing the
  six old schemes; your old choice is carried over. Every text colour is checked for
  readable contrast in every combination.
- No text smaller than 12px, visible keyboard focus everywhere, labels on every control,
  and states shown with words as well as colour.
- Deleting a saved build, clearing the basket or removing an item now asks first.
- The window opens at 1280×900; narrower windows get an icon sidebar, and very narrow ones
  stack everything.
