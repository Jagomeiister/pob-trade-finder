## v2.0.1: new Market page, Gems page, fixes

**Market**
- Covers every item on GGG's currency exchange in your league (~480: currency, fossils,
  essences, oils, scarabs, catalysts, cards and more), not just 14 currencies.
- **Biggest movers** over 24 hours or 7 days. Thin markets are left out because single
  trades swing their price.
- Click any item to chart it, with price and date axes, hover values and traded volume.
  Pick **1 day / 7 days / 30 days**. Change, low, high and typical volume are shown under
  the chart.
- **Watchlist** (☆ any item) and a chaos ↔ divine **converter** for any item.
- A table you can search, filter by category, and sort, with 24h and 7-day change, range,
  volume and trend lines.
- History is now 6-hourly. The first visit downloads about 13 MB from GGG's feed; after
  that it's cached on your PC.

**Gems**
- Laid out like the gear pages: link groups on the left (your main skill ★ opens first,
  with group totals once priced) and the selected group's gems on the right.
  **Price this group** / **Price all gems**, and **▾** on any gem for full listings and
  live search.

**Fixes**
- **Install & restart** no longer shows a ".NET unhandled exception" box. It closed the
  hidden price-check window twice. The update into 2.0.1 still runs 2.0.0's shutdown code,
  so you may see the box one last time.
- **⚙ Settings** (and **Check for updates** inside it) disappeared below the window when
  Text size was above Normal. It's now pinned to the bottom of the sidebar.
- Unique items in the slot list now show their real art instead of the base item's.
- Anoints and other enchants are labelled *enchant*, not *crafted*.
- On wide screens, pages now start beside the sidebar instead of floating in the middle.
