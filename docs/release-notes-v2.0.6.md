## v2.0.6: new themes, menu fix at larger text, clearer messages

**Three new themes** (⚙ Settings → Appearance → Theme)
- **Dim**: dark but not too dark, warm charcoal instead of near-black
- **Slate**: a cool blue-grey dark
- **Parchment**: a warm, low-glare light, easier on the eyes than white

All five themes work with every accent and High contrast, and every combination is
checked for readable contrast.

**Fixes**
- With Text size above Normal, the **⋯** menu and the Search settings popover opened
  off to the side (sometimes past the window edge). They now open right under their button
  at every text size.
- **"Query is too complex"** (weighted or flexible-resistance searches without being
  logged in) is now explained in plain words, with buttons to open the search on the
  trade site, search "all but one" instead, or add a POESESSID.

**POESESSID**
- Still optional and still yours to use. The first time you save one, a short notice
  explains what it is (your logged-in session: never share it), that GGG doesn't
  officially support third-party tools using it, and how to stop. Confirm once and it
  never asks again.
