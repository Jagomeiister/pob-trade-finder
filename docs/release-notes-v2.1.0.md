## v2.1.0: smarter price checks

**Is it an upgrade?** Ctrl+D or paste an item and the card compares it with your build's
item for that slot (both rings for a ring) and with what your imported character wears:
**Upgrade / Sidegrade / Downgrade** with a % and the stat differences. It's a stat
comparison, not a DPS calculation.

**Quick price, no trade search needed**
- Currency, fragments, scarabs, essences and cards are priced instantly from GGG's hourly
  exchange feed, with your stack's value
- Uniques and gems come from poe.ninja through the app's own server (refreshed about every
  30 minutes)
- **Live offers** (exchange items): asks the trade site's bulk exchange who sells it right
  now. It sends one request each time you press it

**Better filters for one item**
- Ctrl+C copies carry mod tiers now (**T1, T2…**). Mods worse than tier 2 start unticked,
  and minimums are 90% of each roll
- **Preset**: **Similar** (those defaults), **Exact** (every mod at full roll, exact base)
  or **Base** (no mods: the base, item level and influence alone)
- New search settings: **Listed within** (skip old, often abandoned listings) and
  **Priced in** (chaos / divine / either)
- ⋯ → **Open on PoE Wiki / PoEDB**

**Listings**
- Seller status dot (online / away / offline) and how long ago each item was listed
- The same seller listing several copies at one price shows once, with **×N**
- Your own listings get a **You** badge (set your account name in Settings)
- Re-checking the same search within 5 minutes uses the earlier result and says how old it
  is. **↻ Refresh** searches again

**Data**: trade stats and unique roll ranges refreshed.
