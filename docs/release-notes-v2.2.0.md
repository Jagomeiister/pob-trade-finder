## v2.2.0: trade helper

**🤝 Trade helper** (Tools → Trade helper, or ⚙ Settings → In game). A small window that stays
on top of the game, with a card for every trade whisper.

- **Selling:** Invite · Busy · Sold · Still interested?. When the buyer arrives the card shows
  **In your hideout** and switches to Trade · Kick · Thanks. Then record the outcome:
  **✓ Completed** (confirm the price), **✗ Not completed**, **⌛ Time wasted** or **Other**.
  "Trade accepted" in the game highlights Completed for you. Invited buyers who don't show up
  can be marked **no-show**
- **▣ Show in stash** outlines the item's cell over your open stash tab (quad tabs too)
- **Buying:** cards for whispers you send, with Visit hideout · Trade · Re-whisper
- **Sales:** every sale in chaos and divines, with totals per league and per day, best sellers,
  average, a chart, filters and CSV export
- **Buyers:** completed trades, total spent, time wasted and no-shows (shown as a badge on their
  next whisper), private notes, flag, hide popups, and **Ignore in game** (sends /ignore once)

**How it plays by GGG's rules:** each button sends exactly one chat command when you press it.
Nothing is sent on a timer or because of something in the log. Invite and Trade are separate
buttons. The app reads Client.txt only after you switch the helper on, and your sales and buyer
notes never leave your PC.
