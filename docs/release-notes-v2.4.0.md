## v2.4.0: skill tree, your own trade sounds, and more

**🌳 Skill tree** (Tools → Skill tree): a mini Path of Building inside the app
- Your build's tree: pan, zoom and search (by node name or stat). Allocated nodes and paths
  are highlighted.
- Jewel sockets show the jewel your build puts in them; masteries show the effect you picked.
- The ascendancy (and bloodline) sits in its own box.
- **Compare with my character** (after "⚖ Compare with character…" once): shows the points
  your character still has to take, in the order you can take them (step 1 = can take now),
  plus any points to refund.

**Trade helper**
- **Your own sounds**: Setup → Sound → "Your own sound…". Use an mp3, wav, ogg or m4a, one
  for a new trade and, optionally, one for when the buyer arrives. Plays at your volume.
- **Show in stash on quad tabs**: the whisper doesn't say whether it's a quad tab, so both
  cells are outlined: amber for a normal tab, blue for a quad tab.
- **One player, several characters**: Buyers → "Same person as…" merges them, so their
  history adds up.

**Tools**
- **Watcher's Eye**: **Price each triple** (jewels with three of your mods), plus **Include
  legacy mods** for old jewels.
- **Div cards**: prices of 1 divine or more show in divines.
- **Search text**: every map mod can now be searched.
