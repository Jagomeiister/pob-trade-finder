## v2.5.0: cheat sheets, a test mode for the trade helper, light-blue crafted mods

- **📜 Cheat sheets** (Tools → Cheat sheets; **Alt+C** shows it over the game):
  - Each act, 1–10: the quests that give a passive point or a respec point, the Trials of
    Ascendancy and the Labyrinth they open, the bandit rewards, and the resistance penalty
    after Acts 5 and 10. The data comes from the Path of Exile Wiki (CC BY-NC-SA).
  - Your own sheets: notes and pictures, for one act or for every act.
  - **Follow my act** (asks first) turns the sheet to the act you're in and marks the trial
    you're standing in. You can also have it pop up when a new act starts.
  - Width, size, opacity, lock and reset position; the hotkey can be changed in Settings → In game.
- **Trade helper → Setup → Test mode**: see what trades look like over the game without a real
  buyer. It makes up whispers, the buyer arriving, "Trade accepted" and more, or plays a
  whole trade. Test cards are marked TEST: their buttons send nothing to the game and nothing
  is saved.
- **Crafted and enchanted mods** are now bright light blue, so they stand out from the other mods.
- **Market**: the change amount shows next to each percentage.
