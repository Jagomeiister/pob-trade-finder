## v2.5.1: gem setups in Compare gear

- **⚖ Compare gear now checks your gems**, wherever they are. Each PoB link group is matched to
  your best link group on any item you wear, weapon swap included, so a setup PoB keeps in the
  body armour still counts when you run it in your helmet ("your Helmet · 4L (PoB has it in
  Body Armour) — 4/4 linked"). Gems in your bag don't count.
  - The Awakened or Vaal version of a build gem counts as having it. The plain gem where the
    build has the Awakened one, or another transfigured version, counts as close.
  - Missing gems, too few links and gems below the build's level or quality are listed.
- **Every gem shows whether it's linked**: *linked · Boots A*, *not linked · Boots B* (same
  item, separate link group), *in Amulet* (another item) or *missing*.
- **Socket picture**: under each group, your items' link groups drawn as socket chains with
  the gem in each socket. Separate groups on one item are lettered A, B…, and the group
  matched to this setup is outlined.
- **Unticked gems in PoB** are now listed under each setup as options. They're left out of
  the group's prices, totals and the compare, and if you run one in place of a ticked gem the
  compare says so.
