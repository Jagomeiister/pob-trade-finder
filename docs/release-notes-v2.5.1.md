## v2.5.1: cleaner in-game overlays, new icons

- **In-game overlays**:
  - Always dark over the game, even with the light theme.
  - One look for every panel. Nearly solid by default (95%), with an outline that stands out on
    bright and dark scenes.
  - Text is a size bigger.
- **Trade cards**:
  - Each card says where the trade is at: Waiting, Invited, In your hideout, Trade accepted or
    Left. The card's coloured edge matches it.
  - "Selling to" / "Buying from".
  - A long name is cut short instead of pushing the ✕ onto its own line.
  - The league only shows when it isn't the one you're playing.
  - The outcome buttons all look the same, with Completed in green.
- **The PoB icon** over the game is bigger. Its hover buttons now include the cheat sheet and
  show their hotkeys.
- **Cheat sheet**:
  - Softer coloured tags.
  - Each trial's trap sits under the area name.
  - The wiki credit is a tooltip on the title in game (the full line is in Tools → Cheat sheets).
- **New icons**: the sidebar and the overlay buttons use a matching set of gold line icons.
  Each name stays next to its icon at every window width.
