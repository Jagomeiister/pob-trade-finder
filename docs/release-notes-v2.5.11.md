## v2.5.11: your character compare comes back after a restart, and settings stay put

- **Compare again when the app starts** (⚖ Compare with character…, on by default): the app
  runs your last character compare once at launch, so the gear marks, the gem socket picture
  and the link tags are there after an update or a restart without pressing Compare gear.
  Same requests as the button, never repeated on its own. Untick it to compare by hand.
- **Fix: in-game settings no longer reset.** The app-over-game, trade-cards and cheat-sheet
  hotkeys, close to tray, "a click in the game puts it away", the see-through setting and your
  chat macros could go back to their defaults after a restart once you changed any other
  setting. They're kept now.
