## v2.5.12: anoint helper in the Blight cheat sheet

- **Anoint helper** (Cheat sheets → Blight): every amulet, ring and blighted-map anoint with the
  oils it takes, shown with their in-game icons, and **what it costs right now** (oil prices
  from GGG's currency exchange).
  - **Search** a notable by name or by what it does ("spell critical").
  - **Sort** cheapest or priciest first.
  - **Pick oils** to see what they make; one oil lists every recipe that uses it. Click a
    row to pick its oils.
  - **Your build's** current anoint is listed first; notables already in your tree are marked.
  - Over the game it's click-only, so it never takes your keyboard.
