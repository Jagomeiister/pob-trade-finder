## v2.5.13: div card value per area

- **Div cards → By area** now ranks areas by **≈ per 1000 drops**: the card value you'd
  expect per 1,000 items dropped there, from community drop weights (the Prohibited Library's
  stacked deck data, via maps-of-exile, MIT).
  - These are estimates, not GGG numbers. Monster density isn't counted, so use it to compare
    areas.
  - **4 voidstones** (on by default) counts maps at area level 83, so cards that need a high
    level are included.
  - Cards without a known weight are left out and flagged (**+N?**).
- Open an area to see each card's odds (≈ one per how many drops) and its share of the
  value.
