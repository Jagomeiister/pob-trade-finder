## v2.5.14: the whole div card on hover

- **Tools → Div cards**: hover a card (its picture or its name) to see the whole card the way
  the game shows it: the name, the full picture with the stack size (e.g. 1 / 8), the reward
  in the game's colours (unique, currency, gem, corrupted, item level…) and the flavour text.
  Before, only a cropped picture showed.
- The reward and flavour text come from the community Path of Exile Wiki and update through
  the app's server between releases, like the drop areas.
