## v2.5.15: loot filter tools

**⏚ Loot filter** (Tools → Loot filter, works without a build) tunes your own .filter file:

- **Price re-tier**: for NeverSink / FilterBlade filters. It finds items whose price today is
  far from their tier (currency, cards, scarabs and more from GGG's exchange feed; uniques from
  poe.ninja), with the tier they fit now. Tick the ones to change.
- **Build items**: highlights your build's rare bases (to craft on), its uniques' bases and
  its gems.
- **Your rules**: hide or show an item class or base types, with a look of your choice or the
  look of any tier in your filter.
- **Find an item**: paste an item to see which block of your filter shows or hides it, with
  **Hide it** / **Highlight it**.
- **Checkpoints**: the app keeps a copy before every save; restore any of them.
- The **Ctrl+D price check** shows which filter block catches the item, with **Hide it** /
  **Highlight it**.

How it changes your filter:
- Everything the app adds is in one section at the top of the file, so it wins without the
  filter's own blocks being rewritten, and comes back after you download a new version of
  your filter (open it, Save).
- The app never touches the game: after saving, pick the filter again in game (Options → UI).
