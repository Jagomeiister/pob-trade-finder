## v2.5.16: Settings in tabs, and a saved POESESSID that says it's saved

- **Settings has tabs** — Look, Alerts, Hotkeys, In game, Account, Updates, Help — and opens
  on the one you used last. **Find a setting** at the top searches every tab at once (a
  setting's name, its help text or its choices).
- Hotkeys and chat macros are together on **Hotkeys**; the app-over-the-game, tray and
  learned-filter options are on **In game**.
- **POESESSID**: it was always kept on your PC between starts, but the box looked empty after a
  restart. Settings → Account now says **Saved on this PC**, has a **Remove** button, tells you
  if a save fails, and explains when pathofexile.com has ended the session (it does when you
  log out there).
