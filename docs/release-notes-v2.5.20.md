## v2.5.20: search text like poe.re

**Tools → Search text** now does what poe.re does, with eleven tabs:

- The search box takes the game's full **250 characters** (it was 50).
- **Maps**: mods to avoid or want, plus at least this much quantity, pack size, rarity, more
  maps, more currency, more scarabs or more div cards.
  - Also quality types, rarity, corrupted, unidentified, map types (Shaper, Elder, Blighted…)
    and a price-note range.
- **Vendor**: 2–6 links, 6 sockets, linked colours in any order (e.g. the chromatic recipe),
  at least N sockets of a colour, levelling mods, item type and rarity.
- **Items**: pick a base (71 groups) and the mods it can roll. Rare: a minimum per mod,
  matched on the rolled value. Magic: the affix names, for alteration crafting.
- **Gems**: pick gems or **Add my build's gems**, with a minimum level and quality.
- **Scarabs**, **Tattoos** (and runegrafts), **Beasts** and **Gwennen** come with this
  league's prices. **Pick the dearest in range** fills the box with the most expensive first.
- **Heist**: contracts by job and level (with a Gianna preset) and by target.
- **Add my own text** after the search, **Copy automatically**, and presets that keep every
  tab. Your old presets still load.
