## v2.5.21: edit your loot filter in game, like Scalpel

Hover an item and press **Ctrl+Shift+D**. The price-check window becomes a filter editor for
that item, next to your inventory or stash, as tall as the game. It edits your filter's own
blocks, and every save reloads the filter in game (turn that off in Settings → Hotkeys).

- **Switch Tier**: every tier of the same kind, each drawn the way the game draws it. Pick
  one and the item moves there. Tiers that can't take it aren't listed.
- **Hide**: says what it will do (hide the tier, or move the item to a hidden tier), then
  does it.
- **The item**: its art, price, item level and stack, plus **Use Zone Level** on a map.
- **Thresholds**: stack size, quality and memory strands as a bar of ranges. Pick a range to
  edit the tier that shows it, or move a boundary.
- **Edit Tier**:
  - the tier's items with prices
  - Show / Hide
  - the label with the font size and **Text / Border / Background** colour pickers (your
    filter's most used colours, hex and RGBA)
  - **Effects**: minimap icon (size, colour, shape), light beam, drop sound with a preview
- **Economy audit**: slide a price and move the tier's dearer items up a tier or the cheaper
  ones down. On unique tiers, by dust too.
- **Uniques on this base**: cards with prices.
- **History**: undo your last 30 edits, restore a saved version, make a checkpoint.
- **Online filters** (FilterBlade, the PoE site): import one to edit a local copy.
  **Check for Filter Updates** fetches the new version; **Update** puts your edits on it and
  switches back. One game command per click.
- Better filter matching for pasted and Ctrl+D items: magic and Superior bases, unusable
  items, map tier, Eldritch implicits, mirrored / fractured / synthesised / transfigured and
  more.
- The game's label font and alert sounds download once, the first time you need them.
