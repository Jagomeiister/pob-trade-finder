## v2.5.23: scarab hub, profit calculator, price boards and pick-and-check helpers

Interactive tools inside the mechanic cheat sheets, with game icons and live prices from GGG's
hourly currency exchange (no trade-site searches).

- **Scarab hub** (Scarabs, Atlas): every scarab with its icon, price, effect and limit per map.
  Build **My setup** (scarabs, map, embers, astrolabes, orbs) and see its cost per map; pick an
  Atlas strategy to fill it in.
- **Profit calculator**: maps per hour, cost per map and expected drops give profit per map and
  per hour, next to the community's own rough estimates.
- **Price boards**: fossils by Delve biome (with the best biome now), essences by tier,
  lifeforce per 1,000, oils, Delirium orbs and Simulacrum, Breach and Legion fragments,
  Expedition currencies, catalysts (Ultimatum, Vendor) and beasts with recipe beasts marked.
- **Pick-and-check helpers**: Eldritch altars, Ultimatum modifiers, Sanctum afflictions and
  Expedition remnants. Tick what your build relies on, click what you see, and get a verdict that
  names anything run-ending for your build, with the reason and its sources.
- **Temple room planner** (Incursion): pick a goal, set your rooms' tiers, see what still needs
  upgrading. Saved.
- **In game** (Alt+C): the boards, checkers and the temple planner are on the sheet's new
  **Tools** tab, click-only.
- The ratings, room values and strategy setups come from community guides and player reports
  (as of 3.29), each checked against its source by three reviewers, and update from the app's
  server like the guides.
