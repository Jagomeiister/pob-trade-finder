## v2.5.24: ready for 3.30 Covens of Umbra, Market like poe.ninja, folding sheets

**Path of Exile 3.30**

- **Atlas tree** and **Skill tree** show the 3.30 trees (the Atlas tree was rebuilt: it starts
  at the centre and has 150 points).
- **Scarabs were removed in 3.30** (Threads replace them). From launch the scarab hub says so,
  and strategy cards mark their scarab lines as removed.
- **Notices**: sheets written for 3.29 say so from launch, until their guide is updated.
  Updated guides open with **Changed in 3.30**. Guide updates arrive from the app's server
  without a new download.
- **Threads** and other new league items are priced as soon as they trade on the exchange.

**Market**

- Laid out like poe.ninja: categories on the left (currency, fragments, essences, fossils,
  uniques, gems, cluster jewels, maps, beasts …), a table per category with value in chaos and
  divines, a 7-day trend, filters and sorting, and a details panel with the chart, alerts and
  a poe.ninja link. The old movers, watchlist, converter and gaps are the **Overview**.

**Cheat sheets**

- **Dropdowns**: every reference table folds, with its row count; the first is open and what
  you open is remembered. The filter opens the tables that match.
- **Bestiary recipe browser**: recipes by item kind (flask, map, weapon, helmet …), each beast
  with its price and the recipe's total; click a beast to see every recipe that uses it.

**Fixes**

- **Saved data is safer**: saves can no longer corrupt the file that holds your builds, trade
  ledger and settings; a backup is kept and used if the file is ever damaged.
- Mechanic tool prices retry after a failed load (with a **Retry** link) instead of staying
  blank for 20 minutes.
- Changing the league now reaches the in-game windows and redraws the open sheet.
- The in-game sheet no longer redraws itself a moment after it opens.
