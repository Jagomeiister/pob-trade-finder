## v2.5.25: Covens of Umbra data, build stages, build guides in the app

**Path of Exile 3.30**

- Leagues, items, stats, uniques and gems are the 3.30 ones (Covens, Hardcore, Ruthless).
- **Threads** have prices and their own **Market** category.
- **Search text**: map totals search **Atlas Drops** and **More Threads**, the 3.30 map lines
  that replaced Item Quantity and More Scarabs.
- Items in builds saved before 3.30 still match their trade stats, although GGG reworded
  215 of them (for example "nearby Allies" became "Allies within 5 metres").
- **Cheat sheets** (arrived from the server already): every mechanic guide opens with
  **Changed in 3.30**, from the patch notes; new **Covens of Umbra** and **Threads and Shaped
  Regions** sheets; 3.30 Syndicate rewards and the new Atziri rooms in the Temple.

**Build stages**

- A build with several gear sets, skill sets or trees in Path of Building gets one **Stage**
  switch at the top (Leveling, Budget, Endgame …). It changes the gear, gems and tree together,
  matched by name, and remembers your pick per build.

**Build guides in the app**

- Save a Mobalytics, Maxroll, Icy Veins, PoE Vault, poe.ninja, pobb.in or Path of Exile forum
  guide link on a build
  (the 📖 button on the build card), or keep a list of guides on the Builds page.
- **Open guide** shows the real page in its own window beside the app, with **Keep on top**.
  The page opens as your own visit to that site; the app doesn't read it, and links to other
  sites open in your browser.
