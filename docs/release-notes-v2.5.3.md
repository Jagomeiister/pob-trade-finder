## v2.5.3: cheat sheets for game mechanics

- **15 mechanic sheets** next to the act sheets (☰ on the sheet, or Tools → Cheat sheets →
  Mechanics): Betrayal rewards per member and division, Blight oils and anoints, Delve fossils,
  Incursion temple rooms, Expedition vendors, Sanctum afflictions, Kingsmarch shipping, Ultimatum
  modifiers, Eldritch altars, the Pantheon (which map boss holds each soul), Vaal Orb outcomes,
  Bestiary recipes, Harvest crafts, vendor recipes and essences.
  - Built from the Path of Exile Wiki (CC BY-NC-SA) and checked against 3.29's changes; rows
    the wiki hasn't updated yet are left out rather than shown wrong.
  - They update from the app's server between releases, so fixes (and 3.30 changes) don't
    need a new download.
- **A small suggestion, not a big window**: when you enter a mechanic's area, a little pop-up
  at the top of the game offers its sheet. **Open** shows it, 🔕 stops it for that mechanic,
  and it goes away by itself after 20 seconds. It's its own piece: drag it anywhere.
- Mechanic tables size themselves to fit, scroll past a max height you can set, and the sheet
  list is click-only so nothing takes your keyboard from the game.
