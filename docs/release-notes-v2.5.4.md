## v2.5.4: socket colours in the gem compare

- **Socket colours** (Compare gear → Gems, under the socket picture). Since 3.29 any gem works
  in any socket, and a gem in a socket of its own colour gets +10% quality. For each of your
  setups it shows:
  - how many gems sit in their colour (e.g. *1 / 4 gems in their colour*);
  - which colours the link group wants;
  - which colours this base rolls (from its attribute requirements);
  - the crafting bench's *At Least 2/3/4 Non-White Sockets* and its cost (plus Vaal Orbs on
    a corrupted item);
  - what a Chromatic Orb does on this item;
  - the Omen of Trichromatism when the setup wants all three colours.

  There are no odds: GGG hasn't published the 3.29 socket colour chances.
