## v2.5.5: poe.ninja links, chat macros, flask and vendor searches, a borderless price check

- **poe.ninja ↗** for an item's price history: in each card's ⋯ menu (uniques, gems, currency,
  divination cards), in each gem's ▾ panel, on the Market chart and on the Ctrl+D quick price.
  It opens in the league you picked.
- **Chat macros** (⚙ Settings → In game): /kingsmarch, /menagerie, /delve and /exit (log out)
  to start with. Change them or add your own, up to 12. One click on ▶ or one press of the
  macro's hotkey sends one command, and the hotkeys only work while the game is focused.
- **Search text → Flasks**: tick the flask mods you want (bleed / freeze / shock / ignite /
  poison immunity, increased effect, reduced charges per use, instant recovery and more).
  Every tier of a mod matches.
- **Search text → Vendor**, for levelling: chromatic recipe (R, G and B linked), 3 / 4 / 5
  linked sockets, movement speed, + level of socketed gems, added spell damage, life.
- **Ctrl+D price check** has no title bar now: drag its banner to move it, and resize it from
  its edges.
- **Fix: the app over the game (Shift+Space)**. Once its title bar was gone, the app could lose
  track of its own window, so dragging the banner, closing with the hotkey and keeping the
  overlays up while it's open could stop working.
