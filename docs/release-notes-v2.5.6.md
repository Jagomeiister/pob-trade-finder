## v2.5.6: set the price you got on the trade card

- **Sold for** on the trade card. Once the buyer is in your hideout, a row above ✓ Completed
  shows the price, starting at the listed one:
  - **−** / **+** change it in steps that suit it (1, 5 or 10 chaos; 0.1, 0.5 or 1 divine);
  - **in div** / **in c** converts it at the current rate if you were paid in the other currency;
  - **Reset** goes back to the listed price.

  ✓ Completed records that price in Sales. No typing needed, so the game keeps your keyboard.
