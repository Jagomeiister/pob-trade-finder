## v2.5.7: dust explorer

- **✨ Dust explorer** (Tools → Dust explorer, works without a build): which uniques are
  worth buying to break down for Thaumaturgic Dust in Kingsmarch.
  - Every unique with its dust, price (the cheapest plain copy on poe.ninja), dust per chaos,
    gold fee, size and how often it's listed, sorted by dust per chaos.
  - Set the item level, quality and extra influences. Hide thin markets or cap the price.
  - Uniques that always come influenced (Starforge, Voidwalker…) already count it.
- **Dust on the price check**: Ctrl+D (or a pasted unique) shows *Dust ≈ …* next to the quick
  price, with dust per chaos, from that item's own item level, quality and influences.
- Dust values: the MIT-licensed poe-disenchant-tool dataset (credited in
  THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
