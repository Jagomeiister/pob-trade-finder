## v2.5.8: deeper cheat sheets, div card art, skill tree icons, saved characters

- **Cheat sheets, much deeper**:
  - **Route** for every act: each zone in order with its level, waypoint, quest steps and
    bosses. With Follow my act on, the zone you're in is highlighted.
  - **Gems**: each quest's gem rewards and what you can buy after, for your build's class,
    with your build's gems highlighted.
  - **24 mechanic sheets** (9 new: Heist, Breach, Legion, Abyss, Delirium, Ritual, Labyrinth,
    scarabs, Atlas map tiers), and more detail in the others: every Blight anoint, Sanctum
    boons, pacts and relics, Expedition factions, Incursion rooms, Delve depths. Content the
    3.29 reworks removed is left out. Long sheets get a filter box in the app.
- **Div cards**: card pictures (hover for a bigger one). The page shows your last prices straight
  away after a restart while it refreshes, and 51 cards whose names have an apostrophe now get
  their prices.
- **Skill tree**: the real passive icons on keystones, notables and small passives as you zoom
  in, in colour for your build and grey for the rest.
- **Compare with character**: ☆ Save a character (yours or a friend's) and compare it with one
  click next time.
- **Updates**: a clearer update banner ("v2.5.8 is ready", what's new, Later, Update now), and an
  **Update now** button right in Settings after Check for updates.
