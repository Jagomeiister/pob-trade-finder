## v2.5.8: a trade ledger — what you bought, profit, flips, people, reports

- **Buying is tracked too.** Cards for whispers you send now end with **✓ Bought** (at the
  **Paid** price, with − / + and *in div*), **✗ Didn't happen**, **⌛ No answer** or **Other**.
  When the game logs "Trade accepted" while you're buying, Bought is highlighted. The seller's
  record with you shows on the card (*sold to you 3× · didn't answer 2×*).
- **Trade helper → Trades**: **Sold**, **Bought** and **Flips**.
  - Earned, spent, net profit and a money in / out chart per day.
  - Currency swaps (e.g. 300 chaos for 1 div) count by how much they gained or lost at
    that hour's rate.
  - **Flips** pairs an item you bought with the same item sold later: profit and how long
    you held it.
  - Sales more than 20% under poe.ninja / the exchange are tagged *under market*.
  - Edit or delete a row, export CSV, and **+ Add trade** for trades done outside the app.
- **Trade helper → People**: buyers and sellers in one list, with filters for **Time
  wasters** (automatic after 2 time wasted / no-show / no answer, or your call), **Flagged**
  and **Blocked** (popups hidden, or ignored from the app) — with **Show popups** and
  **Unignore in game** to undo.
- **Trade helper → Reports**: any league or date range, best day, items you sold (count,
  average, low / high, best buyer), top buyers and sellers, sales under market, and a
  **goal** (e.g. *Mageblood, 40 div*) counting your profit since you set it.
- **Today** line under the in-game trade cards: earned, spent, net and trades today.
- **Setup → Your trade data**: export a backup file, or import one (it only adds what you
  don't have).
- All of it stays on your PC. The only things sent to the game are the buttons you press,
  one command each.
