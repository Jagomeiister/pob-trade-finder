👋 Welcome to PoB Trade Finder
  1. Paste a build: in Path of Building, use Import/Export Build → Generate → Copy, then paste the code (or a pobb.in link) below.
  2. Shop it: pick a slot on the left to see its mods pre-filled. Use Search on trade site, or Check price to see listings here.
  3. Find upgrades: Compare with character shows what each slot is missing.
  4. Jewels: Timeless jewels finds the best seed for your sockets; Jewel finder ranks jewel mods for your build.
  5. In game: hover an item and press Ctrl+D to price it (⚙ settings to change the key).
